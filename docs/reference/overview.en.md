The [Multi-Cloud Platform Engineering Technical White Paper](multi-cloud-platform-engineering-whitepaper.en.md) consolidates four repository boundaries, 40 workflows, GitOps/state/CMDB, Vault paths and permissions, upgrade acceptance, and migration sequencing at the October 5, 2026 baseline. It separates implementation, target design, and pending acceptance.
