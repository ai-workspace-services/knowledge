# Product focus: XConnect provides reliable AI connectivity

XConnect provides connectivity for AI workspaces, including secure access, remote environments, private-network reachability, and real-time collaboration. It connects a workspace to the networks, devices, and services a task needs while keeping the boundary observable.

## Common scenarios

- Access a controlled remote development or compute environment from a browser.
- Give AI tasks reliable access to approved tools and services.
- Carry task state and collaboration results across environments.

XConnect owns connectivity and access; XWorkmate / AI Workspace owns tasks, model calls, and delivery. See the [XConnect product page](https://xworktech.com/products/xconnect) for source and release links.
