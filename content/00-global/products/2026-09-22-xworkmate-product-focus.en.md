# Product focus: XWorkmate brings AI into real work

XWorkmate is XWork Technologies' AI workspace product. It brings models, agents, tools, files, and ongoing tasks into one working context. The goal is not only to generate an answer, but to help users move work toward a deliverable result.

## The problem

Traditional chat windows split conversations, files, and execution results apart. People repeat context, copy intermediate output, and move progress between tools by hand. XWorkmate keeps the working context together so tasks can continue, be reviewed, and be delivered.

## How it works

1. Define the goal, inputs, and acceptance criteria.
2. Connect the models, tools, and source material that fit the task.
3. Let agents execute steps while preserving intermediate artifacts.
4. Review, iterate, and export or deliver the result.

See the [XWorkmate product page](https://xworktech.com/products/xworkmate) for availability, source repositories, and release artifacts.
