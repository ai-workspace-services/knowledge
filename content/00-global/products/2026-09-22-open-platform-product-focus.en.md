# Product focus: Open Platform keeps control of code, data, and deployment

Open Platform is for developers and platform teams that want to self-host, integrate deeply, or extend the system over time. It emphasizes visible source, controlled data, reproducible deployment, and clear engineering boundaries between workspace, connectivity, and infrastructure.

## Who it is for

- Teams deploying an AI workspace in their own environment.
- Teams that need to inspect source, data flows, and service boundaries.
- Teams integrating with existing GitOps, networking, and identity systems.

See the [Open Platform product page](https://xworktech.com/products/open-platform) for architecture, source repositories, and release artifacts.
