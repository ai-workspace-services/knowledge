# Product focus: AI Workspace moves from conversation to delivery

AI Workspace is XWork Technologies' browser-based work entry point. It organizes conversations, tasks, tool calls, files, and delivered results into one continuous flow, so users can start with a goal and carry it through to completion.

## The core flow

Goal definition → context preparation → model and tool calls → review → versioned delivery.

This fits research, development, content production, and operational work that benefits from retained context, repeatable execution, and collaboration. Visit the [AI Workspace product page](https://xworktech.com/products/ai-workspace) for the public product and repository links.
